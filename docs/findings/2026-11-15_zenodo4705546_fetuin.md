# Findings: fetuin 2-AB HILIC-FLD-MS run (Zenodo 4705546)

Run: `MASOP00220_AB_CRL_07JAN2021_Fetuin_001.d` from Smith R., Zenodo 2021,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.4705546 (CC BY 4.0). Agilent HILIC with fluorescence (FLD) and Q-TOF MS.
All numbers below come from the commands listed at the end, run on 2026-11-15.

## 1. Data read

* FLD trace: 15,972 points, 0 to 114.99 min. MS: 9,346 scans, 3.06 to 114.99 min
  (2,050 MS1, 7,296 MS2).
* MS lags FLD by +0.037 min (2.2 s); Pearson r 0.903 over 15 to 50 min.

## 2. Main 2-AB glycans (FLD and MS)

* 31.7 min: Hex6HexNAc5NeuAc2 2-AB, neutral 2707.99 Da (+2.9 ppm); 2+ and 3+ agree within 2 ppm.
* 36.8 min: Hex6HexNAc5NeuAc3 2-AB, neutral 2999.08 Da (+1.6 ppm).
* MS2 of 1355.00, 903.67 and 1000.70 shows the 2-AB Y1 ion (GlcNAc+2-AB, 342.166) at 24 to 30%
  of base peak.

## 3. Non-fluorescent reducing-end series (+43.0786 Da)

* MS-only peaks at 30.7 and 35.8 min: neutral 2630.9995 and 2922.0940 Da, one NeuAc apart.
  No glycan composition (Hex, HexNAc, dHex, NeuAc, NeuGc; free, 2-AB or reduced; with acetyl,
  sulfate, phosphate or water loss) fits within 5 ppm.
* MS2 of 1316.51, 878.01, 1462.05 and 975.04: glycan oxonium ions (204.087, 366.140, 292.103,
  274.092) present; Y1 at 265.17 (GlcNAc + 43.08) present at 2 to 12% of base; 2-AB Y1 (342.166)
  absent.
* Interpretation: sialylated glycans carrying a reducing-end group of net +C3H9N-O
  (+43.0786 Da) instead of 2-AB. Predicted m/z 1316.5042, 878.0052, 1462.0519, 975.0370;
  observed +0.9 to +3.2 ppm, consistent with the run's +2 ppm offset on 2-AB glycans.
* Across 8 non-fucosylated compositions, tag/2-AB MS1 area ratio 0.350 to 0.461 (pooled 0.436);
  the tag series carries 30.4% of tag plus 2-AB MS1 signal. Tag species elute 0.48 to 1.15 min
  before their 2-AB forms. MS signal is not molar abundance.
* Possible origin (not tested): the record states unreacted 2-AB was removed by acetone
  precipitation after cyanoborohydride labelling; reductive amination with acetone can add an
  isopropyl group.

## 4. False "free fucosylated" glycans from sodium-adduct isotopes

* Every free + dHex composition co-elutes with the 2-AB form of the same composition without
  dHex, where fluorescence is high.
* free + dHex [M+zH] lies 1.9 to 3.0 ppm from the M+4 isotope of the 2-AB glycan's
  [M+(z-1)H+Na] ion, for z = 2 and 3 and all six compositions tested
  (dHex - 2-AB = 25.989 Da; Na - H + 4 x 13C spacing = 25.995 Da).
* A composition search including free glycans would report six fucosylated free glycans that
  are isotope peaks of sodium adducts.

## 5. Evidence layers on the full run (476 compositions, 392 formulas, 3 end groups)

* Main 2-AB glycans (8) unflagged. C3H9N-RA detected as a side-product series of 2-AB
  (n=20, area ratio median 0.381, MAD log10 0.060, shift -0.91 min, all same sign,
  fluorescence 0.48 of expected).
* In-source fragments: Hex5HexNAc5NeuAc1, NeuAc2, NeuAc3 (2-AB) co-elute with Hex6HexNAc5NeuAc2,
  NeuAc3, NeuAc4 and are lighter by Hex + NeuAc (453.148 Da).
* Isotope shadow: compositions differing by dHex + Hex - NeuGc (1.0204 Da) share signal within
  the 10 ppm window.
* Ambiguous: Hex5HexNAc4NeuAc3 2-AB vs Na adduct of Hex9HexNAc5 2-AB (5.2 ppm apart).
* Candidate-area shares are search-space dependent and are not reported as census results.
